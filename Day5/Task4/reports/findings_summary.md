# Employee Salary Analysis – Findings Summary

## Problem
The objective of this project is to analyse employee salary data to understand salary distribution, compare departments and identify useful business insights.

## Data
The dataset contains 8 employees with information about employee ID, name, department, salary and city. Missing values were handled during the data cleaning process.

## 3 Key Insights
1. **Overall salary:** The average employee salary is ₹60,375.
2. **Department comparison:** Finance has the highest average salary at ₹90,000, while Sales has the lowest average salary at ₹41,000.
3. **City distribution:** Chennai has the highest number of employees, with 5 employees.

## 2 Recommendations
1. Review salary differences between departments to understand whether compensation is consistent with job roles and responsibilities.
2. Use employee distribution across cities to support workforce planning and future hiring decisions.

## Limitations
- The dataset contains only 8 employees, so the findings may not represent a larger organisation.
- Missing values were filled using statistical methods, which may affect results.
- The dataset does not contain job titles, experience, performance ratings or historical salary data.
- Employee ID is an identifier and cannot be used to establish meaningful business relationships.

## Conclusion
The analysis provides a basic understanding of employee salaries, department-level salary differences and employee distribution across cities. A larger dataset with additional employee attributes would support more detailed analysis.
